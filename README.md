<h2>Hey, I’m Aritra.</h2>

I’m a data analyst with a computer science background and graduate training in applied analytics and econometrics.  
I focus on turning messy, real-world data into reliable analysis, models, and outputs that can actually support decisions.

I care less about flashy techniques and more about:
- whether the data pipeline holds up  
- whether assumptions are clear and defensible  
- whether the output is usable beyond a notebook  

Most of my projects involve owning the full flow - from raw data and problem framing to modeling, validation, and final insight.

<h3>What I Work On</h3>

- End-to-end analytics pipelines (ingestion -> cleaning -> modeling -> output)
- Predictive modeling and ranking problems
- Causal and econometric analysis for real-world questions
- ML and GenAI workflows for classification, detection, and automation

<h3>How I Work</h3>

- I’m methodical, but practical - not academic for the sake of it  
- I start by clarifying the problem and the decision it’s meant to support  
- I document tradeoffs, assumptions, and limitations instead of hiding them
